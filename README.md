# Engel Curves & Industry Growth

Evidence-led research on how household spending shifts with income and the implications for industry growth and equity prices across major economies.

## Stack

- **Next.js 14** (App Router, standalone output)
- **Tailwind CSS**
- **Recharts** — interactive charts
- **TypeScript**

## Sections

1. Economic mechanism (necessities, luxuries, inferior goods)
2. Income trajectories — World Bank PPP 2000-2024 (5 economies)
3. Engel curve scatter — food share vs. GDP per capita
4. China 2023 NBS spending breakdown + income elasticities
5. Cross-economy evidence table
6. 4 ranked industry chains with falsifiers
7. China scenario tree (3 branches)
8. Data gaps log

## Deploy

Railway auto-detects Next.js via nixpacks. Config in `railway.json`.

## Data provenance

Every claim is labeled: `[OBS]` primary source · `[INF]` model inference · `[JDG]` analyst judgment · `[GAP]` evidence unavailable.
